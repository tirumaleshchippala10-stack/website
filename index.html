HOW TO MAKE A TEA

STEPS TO MAKE A TEA

1.BUY A MILK PACKET.

2.TAKE A BOWL.

3.TURN ON YOUR GAS-STOVE

4.KEEP THAT BOWL ON GAS STOVE AND TURN GAS ON AND LIGHT THE GAS WITH A MATCH STICK.

5.POUR THE DESIRED AMOUNT OF MILK INTO THE BOWL.

6.ADD SOME WATER IF YOU WANT LITTLE BIT OF SMOOTH CONSISTENCY.

7.STIR IT WI A SPOON FOR SOME TIME TO SETTLE.TH

8.ADD TEA POWDER INTO MILK .

9.WAIT UNTL THE MILK FOAM STARTS RISING TO TOP OF THE BOWL.

10.STIR AGAIN WITH SPOON.

11.TAKE IT TO A CUP AND YOU ARE GOOD TO GO.
